About the Clinic

Brian Murphy, MISCP
Chartered Physiotherapist

Portumna Physiotherapy & Sports Injury Clinic was established in July 2002 and has been providing expert musculoskeletal and sports injury care to patients across County Galway and the wider Midlands region ever since.

The clinic is custom-built, fully wheelchair accessible, and equipped with modern treatment facilities and private parking. Brian places particular emphasis on accurate diagnosis, thorough patient education, and evidence-based treatment.

Patients of all ages are welcomed, from children with developmental conditions to older adults managing long-term musculoskeletal issues.
